Features

Interactive Transcripts

Follow along with automatically synced subtitles (SRT/VTT). Click any word to jump to that exact moment in the video or audio.

Echo Mode

Practice speaking with line-bounded shadow reading. Listen, record, and compare your pronunciation.

YouTube Support

Learn from any YouTube video with dual-engine playback and integrated transcripts.

Dictionary Lookup

Instant definitions and translations without leaving the player. Build your vocabulary seamlessly.

Cross-Platform Sync

Your progress, library, and settings sync across Windows, macOS, Android, and iOS.

Pronunciation Scoring

See accuracy, fluency, completeness, and prosody scores for every shadow-reading take.

Spaced-Repetition Vocabulary

Save words in context and review them with a vocabulary deck built around your media.
